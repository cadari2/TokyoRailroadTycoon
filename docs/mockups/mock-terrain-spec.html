Water · Kaidō · Credit — visual & rules spec

Water bodies ／ 水系

Three distinct water types plus swamp. Rivers are 1 hex wide and always run mountain → valley → sea. Sea fills the southeast (Tokyo Bay); lakes are inland pools where a river crosses a basin. Only rivers/moats/canals are bridgeable; only sea and lakes are reclaimable.

Kaidō → Highway ／ 街道

The five random road spokes are replaced by four named government corridors that persist all game in the same hexes — Tōkaidō (SW, to Yokohama), Kōshū Kaidō (W), Nikkō/Ōshū Kaidō (N, sharing the Senju approach), — routes jitter per seed but keep their historic start bearing out of Nihonbashi. Rights to build on a corridor hex are purchased from the government.

1872 — 1945
Dirt kaidō 土の街道

Packed-earth post road. Cheap crossing rights; slight dev bonus along it.

1945 — 1960
Paved road 舗装道路

Hexes convert gradually from the city outward. Rights cost more; road competes for riders (mode share).

1960 — 2028
Highway 高速道路

Full conversion. Strong car competition in its corridor; crossing rights expensive (viaduct).

Loans & failure ／ 借入と破産

One instrument, era-flavored: a bank credit line sized by your assets and player class. Interest accrues monthly. Taxes unpaid for 3 straight years force borrowing; if the credit line can't cover it, the company is sold out and the game ends in failure.
